# **Who We Are**

**StoryBridge Content & Media was born from a friendship, a shared newsroom, and years of experience turning ideas into stories.**

We are **Assia Touati and Imen Bliwa**, two best friends who first met through work. What began as a professional relationship quickly became a friendship built around a shared passion for journalism, communication, language, and good storytelling.

Imen is a journalist, translator and researcher working across **Arabic, English and French**, with more than a decade of experience in journalism, translation and international media. Her work has taken her across different subjects, audiences and formats, from journalistic reporting and research to translation and content development.

Assia is an **open-minded editor-in-chief** with a strong editorial instinct and a talent for turning ideas into clear, engaging and meaningful content. Her experience in editorial work brings another essential perspective: understanding the audience, shaping the message and making content work as part of a larger product.

We first worked together at a magazine where we experienced something particularly valuable: **building a successful media product from the inside.** We learned how an idea becomes a story, how stories become a product, and how strong editorial work, communication and teamwork can create something people actually connect with.

Then our professional paths took us in different directions.

But our friendship didn't.

We kept meeting at one of our favorite coffee places, at our offices, or simply whenever we had time to sit down and talk. We talked about our work, the media industry, businesses, communication, languages and all the things we thought could be done differently.

And eventually, one idea kept coming back:

**Why not build something together?**

That idea became **StoryBridge Content & Media.**

### **What we do**

StoryBridge Content & Media is a **strategic communications and multilingual content company** helping businesses, organizations, media platforms and professionals communicate clearly across languages and audiences.

Our services are built around four core areas:

**• Content & Editorial**  
Articles, website content, social-media content, branded content and editorial material.

**• Translation & Localization**  
Arabic, English and French translation, with attention to meaning, context, tone and audience.

**• Editing & Writing**  
Editing, proofreading, rewriting and improving existing content while preserving its purpose and voice.

**• Media & Press Services**  
Press coverage, journalistic content, media-related support and communication materials.

These services build directly on the original project plan, which identified professional writing, editorial work, Arabic/French/English translation and journalistic content as the core offering.

### **Why StoryBridge Content & Media?**

Because language is more than translation.

A good translation does not simply move words from one language to another. A good article does not simply fill a page. And good communication is not simply about saying more.

**It is about making the right message reach the right people in the right way.**

That is the bridge we want to build.
